# Products and Variants are archived, not deleted, and Order Items copy the price paid

A Product or Variant that has ever been ordered is never deleted. It is marked Archived and disappears from the shop and from Carts. Order Items store the price paid (and the Product name and Variant label at Checkout), so later edits, price changes or archiving never alter past Orders, Earnings or Reviews. Only a never-ordered item may be hard-deleted.
