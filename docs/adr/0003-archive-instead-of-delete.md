# Products and Variants are archived, not deleted, and Order Items copy the price paid

A Product or Variant that has ever been ordered is never deleted. The Admin marks it Archived instead, and it disappears from the shop and from Carts. Order Items store the price paid, the Product name and the Variant label at the time of the Order, so later edits, price changes or archiving never alter past Orders, Revenue or Reviews. Only a never-ordered item may be hard-deleted.
